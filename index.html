      About your data

      Entries and the currency choice stay in this browser on this device, under the localStorage key tradingJournal.v1. They are not uploaded and they are not synced to another browser, phone, or computer.

      Clearing site data or the cache deletes them. A private or incognito window starts empty, and its entries disappear when that window closes. Browsers limit this storage to about 5 MB.

      Export a CSV regularly and keep that file as your backup. Opening this page as a file and opening it from a local server are different stores, so they do not share entries.

      CSV columns: date, trades, profit_loss, currency, note. Import adds rows. Amounts are not converted.
